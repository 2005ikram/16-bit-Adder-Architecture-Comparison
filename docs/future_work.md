The current project focuses on the functional verification and timing comparison of 16-bit RCA and CLA using a simplified unit-delay model.
Possible future improvements include :
-More realistic delay modeling .
-Comparison with other adder architectures(CSLA,CSKA,CICA).
-FPGA implementation and hardware testing.
-Graph-based timing analysis to visualize critical paths and signal propagation.
-Exploration of CMOS-level implementations for a more realistic ASIC -oriented analysis.
